## 5. Proposed approval separation

The proposer, resource owner, security decision maker,
independent reviewer, and evidence custodian must have
explicitly documented responsibilities.

An independent approval may not be supplied by the same
actor whose action requires independent review.

A GitHub PR approval confirms review of the change
presented in that PR. It does not automatically mean
that operational security or governance authority
has been delegated to the reviewer.

Future decisions must identify:
- Decision identifier and version.
- Proposing actor and accountable owner.
- Exact environment and resource scope.
- Permitted and explicitly excluded activities.
- Independent reviewer and decision outcome.
- Supporting evidence and known limitations.
- Approval timestamp and validity conditions.
- Revocation, expiry, or reapproval triggers.

The authorized approval procedure is not yet established.

## 10. Proposed decision and approval procedure

Step 1: An authorized process identifies candidates
for each accountable governance role.

Step 2: The resource and security authorities
confirm the intended staging-only scope.

Step 3: Data-governance and policy owners propose
retention, deletion, audit, and custody requirements.

Step 4: Required independent reviewers evaluate
the exact proposal and segregation of duties.

Step 5: An authorized decision record documents
approvals, restrictions, unresolved issues,
and clearly identified responsible parties.

Step 6: Any subsequent operational authorization
is evaluated separately against the applicable
security and production boundaries.

These are proposed procedural requirements only.
None has been completed by this document.

## 13. Stop conditions

Stop if:

- Self-approval substitutes for required review.
- An accountable governance owner is missing.
- Retention or deletion policy remains unresolved.
- Audit oversight responsibility is unknown.
- Evidence storage responsibility is undefined.
- Approval procedures are not independently reviewed.
- An approval record omits its exact scope.
- A staging decision includes production resources.
- Sensitive evidence lacks custody protections.
- Proposed access exposes secrets or personal data.
- A merged proposal is treated as authorization.
- A GitHub review is treated as an Azure role grant.
- Live Azure, RBAC, Graph or storage operations
  are requested without separate authorization.

Any unresolved stop condition requires HOLD.
